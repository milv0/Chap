# Chap 2.7.0

## Added

- **Seal menu bar icon** — Pick Chap the Seal as your menu bar icon in
  Settings > General. It turns Chap blue while Keep Mac Awake runs.

## Changed

- **Meet Chap** — The pixel seal has a name, and it's Chap.
- **A real Chap on button** — Pick 1h, 4h, or 8h (Chap remembers your pick) and
  press one big blue Chap on button. While it runs you see the time left, a bar
  of how much remains, and a quiet Chap off.
- **The seal dives in** — Press Chap on and the seal hops, splashes into the
  water, and pops back up, then wags its tail while Focus runs.
- **One row style** — Sites, Finder, and Downloads rows share one clean look,
  and Downloads is wider so file names show in full.
- **Tidy empty shelves** — With no screenshots, the Screenshots slot folds away
  on its own and returns when you take one.
