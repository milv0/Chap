# Chap 2.3.0

Quick Note moves to the notch's top strip, and Chap Drop learns to share.

## Added

- **Share dropped files** — Hover a file in Chap Drop and click the share button
  to send it with AirDrop, Messages, Mail, and more. Right-click a file for Open,
  Share…, Show in Finder, and Remove.

## Changed

- **Quick Note in the top strip** — Quick Note is now a note icon next to Mirror.
  Click it and the note opens just below, ready to type. Hide it with Show Quick
  Note in Settings → Notch.
- **Click away to close** — Clicking outside an open Quick Note or Mirror, or
  switching apps, folds it away.
- **Tidier top strip** — Mirror sits right next to the Drop box, and the open
  notch always shows the box, even when it is empty.
- **Minimum notch width** — The open notch is at least 640pt wide and centers its
  widgets, so a few widgets no longer squeeze it against the notch.
- **Screenshot times on the right** — Each screenshot row shows its time at the
  right edge.
