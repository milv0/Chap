# Chap 2.5.0

## Added

- **Chap the Seal** — Chap's pixel mascot, a baby seal, rests on the left of the
  notch's black strip and flicks its tail when you open the notch.
- **Seal in Focus** — The Focus slot shows the seal: it sleeps while Focus is off,
  wakes and wags its tail while Focus runs, and gets drowsy in the last 30
  minutes. It holds still with Reduce Motion on.

## Changed

- **Scrollable shelves** — Screenshots and Downloads keep up to twelve items.
  Four show at a glance; scroll for the rest. No scroll bar, just a soft fade.
- **Centered shelf folders** — Clicking the Screenshots or Downloads title opens
  that folder at the Standard size, centered on the screen with the pointer.
- **Slimmer Downloads slot** — Smaller file names in a narrower slot keep the
  dock compact.
- **Tidier strip** — While the Focus slot is showing, the strip's left side
  stays empty instead of repeating the timer.
