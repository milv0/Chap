Why Chap?

Chap! Straight to it.

The name comes from a seal's quick chap-chap call. Just as a seal glides swiftly through water, Chap uses one Option shortcut to take you straight to an app, URL, folder, or script.

Product history

A curated record of releases that changed how Chap works, not every patch.

v1.1.0

Reuse the Chrome window already open

Each URL can now bring its matching Chrome window forward and place it on the configured display instead of opening another window.

v1.0.10

Pause Option-key triggers when needed

General settings added a trigger switch, status bar icon controls, and focused settings navigation while keeping launchables close at hand.

v1.0.5

Secure updates inside Chap

Manual update checks arrived with Sparkle 2, signed update verification, and a fail-closed update feed.

v1.0.2

First public release

Chap launched with URL, app, Finder, and shell shortcuts, multi-display placement, and notarized macOS installers.
