A quiet command surface

More capability. Less chrome.

Chap 2 treats the notch as hardware, not decoration. It expands only when you ask, preserves the native menu fallback, and adapts to your display setup.

01 · NOTCH LAUNCHER

Four slots. Your order.

Drag Sites, Apps, Folders, Scripts, or Screenshots into the Notch settings board. Each launcher type stays intentionally capped at four.

02 · EXACT PLACEMENT

Every window lands where intended.

Choose a display and size preset. Chap centers the window, reads the Accessibility bounds back, and corrects apps that clamp their size.

03 · LIQUID GLASS

Clear by day. Regular by night.

On macOS 26+, Apple Liquid Glass uses semantic text, Clear/Regular materials, and System/Light/Dark appearances. Custom color remains available everywhere else.

Chap Drop

Move files through the notch.

Drag a file toward the notch. The launcher becomes a translucent Drop here surface, copies the file into Chap’s private Application Support folder, and adds it to the dock’s Finder-style file row. Drag it out when you need it.

Product history

The releases that changed Chap.

The notch becomes a command surface
