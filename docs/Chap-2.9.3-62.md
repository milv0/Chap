# Chap 2.9.3

## Added

- **⌥L for Lights Out** — Press ⌥L anywhere to cover your screens. Change the key
  or clear it to turn it off in Settings → General → Lights Out. A site or Quick
  Note already using the key keeps it, and Settings says so.
- **Chap naps for real** — On the Lights Out cover the seal now sleeps: z's drift
  up one at a time, its brightness rises and falls like breathing, and now and
  then it flicks its tail as if dreaming. It holds still with Reduce Motion on.

## Changed

- **One-line shortcut settings** — The Quick Note and Lights Out shortcuts are a
  single row each; the explanation moved to the tooltip.
