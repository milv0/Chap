# Chap 2.16.3

## Fixed

- **Even Sites and Finder lists** — Sites and Finder rows now line up with the
  To-do rows, with the same space above and below, instead of leaving a gap
  at the bottom.

## Changed

- **To-do fits your words** — The To-do slot widens to fit your longest item, up
  to a point, and narrows when the list is short. It settles each time you open
  the notch, so nothing shifts while you type.
- **Tidier app grid** — Up to four apps sit in a 2 × 2 grid; five or six use
  three columns.
- **Simpler pop-up** — The Time's up and Keep Awake pop-ups show Chap right on
  the card, without the blue box around it.
