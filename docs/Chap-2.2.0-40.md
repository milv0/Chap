# Chap 2.2.0

A cleaner, more capable notch.

## Added

- **Mirror** — A webcam icon in the black strip beside the notch. Click it and
  your camera, flipped like a mirror, pops open just below the strip so you can
  check yourself before a call. Nothing is recorded, and Chap asks for camera
  access only the first time. Hide it with Show Mirror in Settings → Notch.
- **Quick Note** — Type a plain-text note right in the notch. It saves as you
  type and keeps the notch open while you write; press Esc or click elsewhere
  to finish.
- **App icons** — The Apps widget shows your apps as a two-column icon grid,
  and you can now add up to six apps.
- **Edit from the notch** — Click the Sites, Apps, or Finder title to open that
  list in Settings.

## Changed

- **More legible notch** — Fewer text sizes, brighter headings, stronger
  shortcut keys, aligned rows, and columns sized to their content. Screenshots
  show when they were taken instead of a cut-off file name, and dropped files
  appear as a compact row.
- **Cleaner shortcut keys** — The notch shows just the key (1, N). Hold Option
  while it is open to light them up as ⌥1, ⌥N.
- **Plain app launch** — Clicking an app without a shortcut in the notch opens
  it as-is. Apps with a shortcut, Sites, and Finder still open centered at their
  saved size.
- **Six notch slots in one row** — The page dots and swipe from 2.1 are gone;
  widgets on later pages move into free slots automatically.
- **Finder naming** — The notch section formerly called Folders is now Finder.
- **Easier adding in Settings** — Click the URL, App, or Finder heading, then +
  to add an item to that section.
- **Glass** — Clear Glass gets a light veil for contrast, and new setups default
  to Regular Glass. Your saved choice is kept.

## Notes

- macOS 14.0+ is required. Liquid Glass requires macOS 26+.
- Camera permission is needed only if you use Mirror.
