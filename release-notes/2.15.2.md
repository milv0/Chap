# Chap 2.15.2

## Changed

- **One-line Launchers tabs** — The Launchers title is now the tab bar itself:
  the tab you're on shows its icon and name, and the others show just an icon
  (▢ Sites  ■  ▤). Click an icon to switch; click the current one again to open
  its settings. The same icon no longer appears twice, so the slot is narrower.
