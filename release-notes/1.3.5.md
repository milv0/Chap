# Chap 1.3.5

## Added

- **Persistent shortcut hints** — Settings > General now always shows
  ⌥ . (open menu), ⌥ , (open Settings), and ⌥ (key) (launch site) so you
  don't need to check Q&A to recall them.

## Improved

- **No more scrollbar in site settings** — Increased the settings window's
  minimum height and hid the scroll indicator, so the site configuration
  panel no longer shows a scrollbar.
- **About Chap moved** — Removed "About Chap" from the menu bar list;
  click the app icon at the bottom of Settings > General to open it instead.

## Notes

- macOS 14.0+ required. Chrome required for URL launch type.
