# Chap 2.14.1

## Changed

- **Focus & Timer, one dial** — Timer now lives in the Focus notch slot. Click
  the timer icon in its title row, drag the same dial to choose 5–60 minutes,
  then press Start. Focus keeps its 1–12-hour Keep Mac Awake dial.
- **Keeps your layout** — An existing Timer slot becomes a Focus slot when Chap
  updates. If you already placed Focus, the old Timer position becomes empty so
  your other slots do not move.
