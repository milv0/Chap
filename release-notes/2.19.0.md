# Chap 2.19.0

## Added

- **Fresh downloads show up** — If you keep the Downloads shelf folded, it now
  opens by itself when you open the notch within 10 minutes of a download, then
  folds back the next time after that. Your folded setting stays as it is. Fold
  it again and it waits for the next download.
- **Welcome back** — When you lift Cover Screens, Chap greets you with
  "Welcome back" for a moment in the middle of the screen.
