# Chap 2.6.0

## Added

- **Collapsible shelves** — Collapse Screenshots or Downloads from the chevron at
  the end of its title (or right-click → Collapse). It folds into a small white
  icon on the left of the notch's black strip, across from Drop, Mirror, and
  Quick Note, and the dock narrows to fit. Click the icon to bring it back.
- **Full download names** — Rest the pointer on a cut-off name in Downloads to
  see it in full, right where it is.

## Changed

- **Panel color to the corners** — In every style the black strip fades out
  before the dock's top corners, so your panel color meets the top of the screen.
- **Calmer dock** — No more lines between slots; a little more room and each
  slot's title keep them apart.
- **No strip countdown** — The black strip no longer shows the Focus timer. The
  seal stays awake and wags its tail while Focus runs; the time left is in the
  Focus slot and the status menu.
