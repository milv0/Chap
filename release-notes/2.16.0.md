# Chap 2.16.0

## Changed

- **Apps and Workspaces share a slot** — The Apps slot now has two tabs: your
  app icons and your Workspaces. Both are icon tiles, so the slot keeps its shape
  when you switch. Apps now sit in three columns, two rows.
- **Launchers is lists only** — Launchers keeps Sites and Finder. Apps moved back
  to their own slot, where an icon grid fits without empty space beside it.
- **Every widget fits** — Screenshots, Downloads, Launchers, Apps, Focus, and
  To-do fill the six slots exactly.

## Removed

- **Separate Sites, Finder, and Workspaces widgets** — They live on as tabs.
  Your layout updates in place: the first Sites or Finder slot becomes Launchers,
  a Workspaces slot becomes Apps, and any slot left over turns empty.
