# Chap 2.8.6

## Added

- **The notch launcher works without a notch** — On a Mac with no notch, point
  at the top middle of the menu bar and the same dock opens. Nothing is drawn up
  there until you do, so the menu bar stays clear and its clicks still belong to
  your apps.

## Fixed

- **The Focus HUD seal holds still** — The z no longer pushes the seal down when
  a session ends; it fills the margin above it, so starting and ending a session
  show Chap in the same place. The overlay appears on the built-in display with
  the notch.
