# Chap 2.4.2

## Changed

- **Mist panel preset** — Custom panel color presets are now Black and Mist, a
  soft blue-gray light. Any other color is still one click away in the picker.
- **White strip widgets** — The Drop, Mirror, and Quick Note icons in the notch's
  black strip are white again; only an open tool turns blue. Section icons keep
  the soft Chap blue.
