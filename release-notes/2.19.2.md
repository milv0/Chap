# Chap 2.19.2

## Added

- **Find your screenshot folder** — Settings → Tools → Screenshots shows which
  folder the shelf reads. **Change…** opens the macOS Screenshot app; pick
  Options → Other Location… to choose or make a folder, and Chap follows along.
  Chap only reads that setting. If screenshots land on the Desktop, Chap lets
  you know other images there join the shelf too.
- **A seal that wanders** — During Cover Screens, the seal on your other
  displays drifts slowly around the screen like a screensaver and bounces off
  the edges. With Reduce Motion on, it stays put in the middle.

## Changed

- **Simpler Glass settings** — System, Light, and Dark are now three icons, sized
  and colored to match the rest of the Appearance section.
