# Chap 2.13.2

## Added

- **Edit a workspace** — Workspaces → Edit Workspace (or right-click a row in the notch) reopens the capture window for that workspace: rename it, leave out windows, or move a window to another display.

## Fixed

- **Removing a workspace works** — Remove Workspace now brings its confirmation to the front instead of opening it behind other apps, and the notch row offers Remove… on right-click.
