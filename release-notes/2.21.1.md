# Chap 2.21.1

## Changed

- **Notch Glow stays on while you work** — The glow now stays around the notch
  for as long as Keep Awake or a timer runs, and fades away when both end. It
  hides while the dock is open.
- **Easy on the battery** — The glow is drawn by the system, so Chap uses about
  1% CPU while it's on.

## Fixed

- **Glow wraps the badges** — When the Drop or timer badge sits beside the
  notch, the glow now goes around it too, with no cut corner or gap.
- **Smooth desktop switches** — The glow now moves with your desktop instead
  of showing up early on the next one.
