# Chap 2.18.0

## Changed

- **Tidier menu bar menu** — Sites, Apps, and Finder now have small headings
  instead of the same icon on every line, and apps show their own icons.
  Cover Screens shows its ⌥ shortcut, and Quick Note is in the menu too, so you
  can find it with the notch off. Layouts has one **Edit Layouts…** item that
  opens Settings instead of two nested lists. The bottom of the menu is one
  quiet group (Settings…, Q&A, Check for Updates…) above **Quit Chap ⌘Q**.
- **One name: Keep Awake** — The notch slot that was called Focus, the menu's
  Keep Mac Awake, and the pop-up now all say **Keep Awake**, so it's clear they
  are the same session (and not macOS Focus). Your last choice on the dial
  carries over. The slot is a little wider so the name fits, and its dial now
  sits in the middle with even space above and below.
