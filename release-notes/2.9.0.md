# Chap 2.9.0

## Added

- **Lights Out** — Step away and cover every screen in black. Turn it on from the
  menu bar; press Esc to come back, or pick another key in Settings > General. If
  you forget the key, pressing any key five times quickly lifts the cover. While
  it is up, Chap keeps the display awake on its own, so it works with or without
  Keep Mac Awake and stays up after a session ends. Nothing you type or click
  reaches the apps underneath, and when you come back the app you were using is in
  front again. It is a cover, not a lock: it asks for no password. Use macOS Lock
  Screen (⌃⌘Q) to secure your Mac.
