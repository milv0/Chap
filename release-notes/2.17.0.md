# Chap 2.17.0

## Added

- **Swipe between tabs** — In the Launchers, Apps, and Focus slots, swipe left or
  right with two fingers on the trackpad to switch tabs (Focus ↔ Timer too). Clicking still works, and now
  the empty part of the title row switches too, so there's no tiny icon to aim
  for. Settings still open only from the ›.
