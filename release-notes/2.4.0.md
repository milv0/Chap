# Chap 2.4.0

Your chap in the menu bar — a friend that opens what you need and lands every
window right where it belongs.

## Added

- **Focus Mode** — A lightning-bolt notch widget for Keep Mac Awake. Chap on
  with 1h, 4h, or 8h; the bolt pulses and a countdown shows how long is left.
  Chap off to stop. It is the same session as Keep Mac Awake in the menu.
- **Downloads** — A notch widget with your four newest downloads, their names,
  and how long ago they arrived. Click to open, drag out, or right-click to
  share or show in Finder. macOS asks once for access to the Downloads folder.
- **Quick Note window** — Pop your note out into its own floating, resizable
  window that stays open while the notch is closed.

## Changed

- **Your chap in the menu bar** — Chap now greets you as a friend, and its words
  follow one voice across the app and the website.
- **Roomier Quick Note** — The note icon turns the widget row into a wide note
  across the notch, with a character count and a copy button.
- **Clearer icons** — Sites use a window icon, and the lightning bolt now means
  Focus, including the Keep Awake timer in the notch.
- **Matching times** — Screenshot times use the same small, quiet style as
  Downloads. Settings now say Show Mirror Icon and Show Quick Note Icon.

## Fixed

- **Notch layout** — The notch grows to fit when Drop files load or Quick Note
  opens, so the bottom row no longer overlaps the widgets.
