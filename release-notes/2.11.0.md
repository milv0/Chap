# Chap 2.11.0

## Changed

- **Lights Out is now Cover Screens** — The name says what it does. The menu
  reads Cover Screens / Uncover Screens, and your ⌥ shortcut and notch icon
  setting carry over.
- **Tap ⌥ to come back** — Uncover your screens by tapping ⌥ Option by itself;
  there is no unlock key to set anymore. Letting go of ⌥ from ⌥L, or holding ⌥
  with another key, does not count. Forgot? Press any key 10 times quickly.
- **Notch Icons** — The Settings → Notch section for the Mirror, Quick Note, and
  Cover Screens icons is now called Notch Icons.

## Added

- **A message on the cover** — Write a line such as "Back in 10 minutes" in
  Settings → Tools → Cover Screens and it shows under the clock while your
  screens are covered.
